# Safe demo recording script

Keep any raw screen captures in the gitignored `demo-private/` directory. Do
not record private recordings, meeting audio, transcript text, signed URLs,
browser developer tools, cookies, or extension/native-host configuration.

## Clip A — generated YouTube subtitles (15–30 seconds)

1. Open a short public YouTube video with clear English speech. The configured
   public smoke video may be used if it remains available.
2. Open Subtitler and choose **Create Subtitles** — not **Use Existing
   Captions**.
3. Show local processing begin, then show one generated subtitle overlay on
   the player.
4. Seek forward and show the same single overlay synchronized at the new
   position.
5. Stop the job once and confirm the overlay disappears. Do not include an ad
   segment in the capture.

## Clip B — local prerecorded transcript (15–30 seconds)

1. Open a non-confidential, authorized local prerecorded media fixture in
   Chrome. Keep the media paused.
2. Choose **Get Full Transcript**.
3. Show that local processing advances while playback remains paused.
4. Show **Transcript ready** and the **View Transcript** control, without
   opening or recording sensitive content.

For a private Webex acceptance run, use the same transcript workflow only for
verification. Never use that page or its output in a public recording.
